# RaceDay ERD Entity Descriptions

## Users

Stores the core account information for users of the system, including their login details, role, and date created.

## Organisers

Stores information specific to users who act as organisers, including their name, contact information, and organisation.

## Participants

Stores information specific to participants, including their name, date of birth, and profile picture information.

## Events

Stores the details of events managed by organisers, including the event name, description, date, 
location, distance, type, and banner image.

## Categories

Stores the categories available within an event. Categories include information such as name, age range, and distance.

## Enrolments

Records a participant's entry into an event and the category selected for that event. It also stores the enrolment date and status.

## Results

Stores the result associated with an enrolment, including finish time, finish position, 
total finishers, and the date the result was captured.
